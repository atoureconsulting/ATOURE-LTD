This period spans two UK financial years (FY2024: 1 Apr 2024–31 Mar
2025, and FY2025: 1 Apr 2025–31 Mar 2026), so profit is time-apportioned
between them, same approach as periods A01 and A02. Actual profit
(£2,911.27 for the whole period) is nowhere near the £50,000 annual
small-profits threshold even before apportioning it — so a single 19%
rate applies to the whole period without needing marginal relief.

## Note on rate — Small Profits Rate vs Main Rate

Same small-profits-rate logic as periods A01/A02 (see
`../2023-09-11_2024-09-10/corporation-tax-workings.md` for the full
explanation of the rate bands and marginal relief). For a 365-day
period, the small-profits threshold itself is effectively the full
£50,000 — actual profit (£2,911.27) is far below this, so the 19%
small profits rate applies cleanly with no marginal relief needed.
