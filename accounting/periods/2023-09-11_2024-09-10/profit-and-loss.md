Under accruals-basis accounting (which applies to limited companies),
these are technically recognised as income when invoiced, then offset by
a bad debt expense once it's clear they won't be collected — netting to
roughly the same tax result as simply excluding them, but this is the
technically correct way to show it on the accounts/CT600. **Confirmed
with user: these are "not looking likely" to be paid — being treated as
bad debts for this draft.** FX conversion for the two €1,500 invoices
still needed for exact figures (roughly £1,280–£1,290 each estimated,
not yet confirmed).

**Net effect: total realised income for period A01 = £2,930.00**, before
expenses. This is the figure that will actually drive the tax
calculation (the bad debts wash out to roughly nil net effect once
matched with their own write-off expense). Compare to HMRC's £38,159
estimate — a difference of over 13x.
