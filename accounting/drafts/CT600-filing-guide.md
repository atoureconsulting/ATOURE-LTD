## Why paper filing, given HMRC's online service is now the default

HMRC's own free online CT600 filing service closed 31 March 2026, and
commercial software (FreeAgent, IRIS Elements Tax, etc.) is now the
normal route. Paper filing is restricted to companies with a
**reasonable excuse** for not filing online, or Welsh-language filers.
Atoure Ltd has an active, HMRC-acknowledged reasonable excuse on file
(the director's hospitalisation, 7–25 Sept 2024, DKA with a new diabetes
diagnosis, ICU admission, discharge 25 Sept 2024) tied to this exact
company tax reference, already the subject of a penalty appeal sent
17 September 2026 and a phone call with HMRC. Given free software access
(via Mettle/FreeAgent) is still pending and the Period 3 deadline
(30 September 2026) is imminent, filing on paper now — with a covering
letter explaining this — is safer than risking a missed deadline. See
`hmrc-covering-letter-paper-filing.md` for that letter.

**Each period needs its own separate CT600 form and its own set of
accounts/computations attached** (box 50 below explains how to flag
this). Use the amended Companies House accounts already prepared
(`../companies-house-filings/Amended-Accounts-Period1-30Sept2024.pdf`
for A01/A02 combined, and
`../companies-house-filings/Amended-Accounts-Period3-30Sept2025.pdf` for
Period 3) as the "accounts" attachment, and the relevant
`corporation-tax-workings.md` + `profit-and-loss.md` pair as the
"computations" attachment (print these too).

attached to each, to:

```
Corporation Tax Services
HM Revenue and Customs
BX9 1AX
```

(This is HMRC's standard postal address for Corporation Tax — no street
address or county needed, per the CT208/CT211 notices already on file.)

three, given the deadline pressure and the amount already at stake —
proof of posting date matters if HMRC disputes timeliness later.
