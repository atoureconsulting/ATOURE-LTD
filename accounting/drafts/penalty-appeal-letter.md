## Notes on this draft

- HMRC generally does **not** accept "didn't know about the deadline" or
  "too busy" as a reasonable excuse on their own — the excuse needs to be
  a specific, unexpected event. Be honest here; an accountant can advise
  on whether there's a real case.
- Even a partially successful appeal (e.g. penalty reduced, or payment
  plan agreed) is worth pursuing — but the biggest lever by far is still
  filing the real return, since it corrects the inflated tax and
  the 10%-of-tax penalty shrinks with it regardless of the appeal outcome.
- Filing the real return is not contingent on this appeal — do both, not
  either/or.
