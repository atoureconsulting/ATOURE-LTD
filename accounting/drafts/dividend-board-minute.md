## Notes on this draft

- This is a written resolution rather than a formal minuted meeting,
  which is standard and valid practice for a sole-director company —
  no separate "meeting" is legally required.
- **Date it truthfully** — the date you actually sign this is the date
  the dividend is legally declared. Do not write an earlier date.
- The dividend is being satisfied by "set-off" against the director's
  loan account rather than an actual cash payment, since the funds
  were already effectively drawn by the director during Period A01.
  This is a completely standard and accepted way to formally clear a
  loan balance with a dividend — no cash needs to change hands again.
- Keep this document with the company's statutory records (it doesn't
  get filed with HMRC or Companies House directly, but needs to exist
  and be produced if ever asked for by HMRC or an accountant/auditor).
- **Recommend accountant review before signing** — particularly to
  confirm the distributable profit calculation and that declaring this
  now, rather than closer to the original period, doesn't cause any
  issue given how much time has passed.
