288 of the Companies Act 2006, the undersigned, being the sole
director of Atoure Ltd, resolved as follows:

**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT:**

1. An interim dividend of **£2,278.00** be declared on the Company's

2. This dividend represents **£22.78 per ordinary share**.

3. The dividend is declared out of the Company's distributable
   (post-tax) profits for the accounting period ended 30 September

   amount currently owed to the Company by Abdul-Malik Baba Toure on
   his director's loan account for this period, reducing that balance
   to nil.

5. A dividend voucher be issued to the shareholder recording this
   dividend.
