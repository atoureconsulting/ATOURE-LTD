**IT WAS RESOLVED THAT:**

1. An interim dividend of **£2,358.13** be declared on the Company's

   which are held by Abdul-Malik Baba Toure.

2. This dividend represents **£23.58 per ordinary share** (rounded;
   £2,358.13 ÷ 100 = £23.5813).

3. The dividend is declared out of the Company's distributable
   (post-tax) profits for the accounting period ended 30 September
   2025, being profit of £2,911.27 less corporation tax of £553.14,
   leaving distributable profit of £2,358.13 — declared in full,
   leaving no retained profit for this period.

4. The dividend is to be satisfied by way of set-off against the
   amount currently owed to the Company by Abdul-Malik Baba Toure on
   his director's loan account for this period, reducing (but not
   eliminating) that balance.

5. A dividend voucher be issued to the shareholder recording this
   dividend.
