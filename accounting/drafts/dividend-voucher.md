## Notes on this draft

- **Keep this document** — you'll need the total dividend figure
  (£2,023.51) when you complete your own personal Self Assessment tax
  return, in the dividend income section. Dividends are taxed on the
  individual receiving them (you), separately from the company's
  corporation tax — there's a tax-free dividend allowance each year
  (check the current year's threshold), and anything above it is
  taxed at dividend rates depending on your other income.
- This is a **standard, simple format** — no special software or
  filing needed. Print or save a signed copy for the company's records.
- If your accountant prefers a different house-style voucher template,
  that's fine — the legal requirement is just that a voucher exists
  showing the shareholder, number of shares, dividend rate, and total,
  dated to match the resolution.
