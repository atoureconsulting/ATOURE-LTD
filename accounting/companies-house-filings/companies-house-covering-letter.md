Dear Sir/Madam,

**Amended accounts for Atoure Ltd — registered number 15129711**

I am writing on behalf of Atoure Ltd, as its sole director, to enclose
two sets of amended accounts, each replacing a set of accounts already
filed for the company:

- Amended accounts for the period ended 30 September 2024, replacing
  the accounts filed on 25 July 2025
- Amended accounts for the period ended 30 September 2025, replacing
  the accounts filed on 20 June 2026

Both original filings were prepared and submitted on my behalf using
figures that do not reflect the company's actual financial position.
On review against the company's bank records, I found that the accounts
filed on 25 July 2025 showed total net assets of £200 with no
traceable basis in any bank statement, invoice, or other company
record, and the accounts filed on 20 June 2026 were prepared on the
wrong accounting basis (full FRS 101 rather than the micro-entity
provisions the company is entitled to use) and included figures —
including £3,000 of trade debtors and £400 of office equipment — that
do not correspond to anything in the company's bank records or trading
activity.

I have since prepared new accounts for both periods directly from the
company's bank statements (Santander, Revolut Business, and Revolut
Personal), reconciled to a single balance sheet across both periods.
Both enclosed amended accounts are marked "AMENDED", state which
filing they replace, and are prepared as they stood at the date of the
original accounts, in accordance with your guidance on correcting
filed accounts. Both are prepared in accordance with the micro-entity
provisions of the Companies Act 2006, which the company is entitled to
use, and for which the company was entitled to exemption from audit
under section 477.

I would be grateful if these amended accounts could replace the
originals on the public record. Please let me know if any further
information or a specific form is required to complete this correction.

Yours faithfully,

Abdul-Malik Baba Toure
Director, Atoure Ltd
